# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

FocusBrief AI is a Jira application developed by an Independent Developer. It helps teams review Jira tasks, identify deadlines and blockers, understand priorities, and generate daily work briefings.

This Privacy Policy explains how FocusBrief AI accesses and processes information when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Access

Depending on how you use the application, FocusBrief AI may access:

- Your Atlassian display name, timezone, and avatar URL.
- Jira issue keys, summaries, statuses, priorities, due dates, project information, and related issue information.
- Relevant Confluence documentation summaries when the Confluence feature is used.

The application accesses Atlassian information through authenticated APIs and respects the permissions of the user using the application.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by FocusBrief AI is used to:

- Generate daily work briefings.
- Identify approaching deadlines and overdue tasks.
- Detect potential blockers and issue dependencies.
- Calculate explainable task priority scores.
- Find relevant Confluence documentation.
- Present recommendations that help users organize their work.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

The current application implementation does not use a separate application-managed database or persistent storage system to retain the Jira or Confluence information described above.

Information is processed to generate the requested briefing and is returned to the application interface. The application does not intentionally retain this information in an application-managed cache for longer than 24 hours.

Atlassian may process operational data and application logs through its own infrastructure in accordance with its applicable policies.

## 5. Third-Party Services

FocusBrief AI operates within the Atlassian Forge platform and uses Atlassian Jira and, where applicable, Confluence APIs.

The application does not intentionally send accessed Jira or Confluence information to external AI providers, advertising networks, or independent analytics services.

Atlassian's own services remain subject to Atlassian's privacy and security policies.

## 6. Security

The application uses Atlassian's authenticated platform APIs and permission model. Access to Jira and Confluence information is limited by the permissions granted to the user.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. User Control

You can stop using FocusBrief AI by uninstalling it from your Atlassian site. Access to information through the application is subject to the permissions and controls provided by Atlassian.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the customer support email address displayed on the FocusBrief AI Atlassian installation or distribution page.
